\subsection{A simple paired local calibration}

Equation~\eqref{eq:local-calibration} implements a small post-fit change to
WGAN-DML. The architecture, adversarial loss, three folds, 1,200 training
steps, and 50,000 generated maxima per fold are unchanged. A fixed pseudo-count
of 50 gives the WGAN about 13.0, 3.6, and 0.74 percent weight as $n$ increases.
The comparison uses ten new repetitions per sample size, with base seed
20260928, following a one-sample development check with seed 20260927.
All variants share the data, folds, and trained generators. Every repetition
is retained; no estimator uses the known target in training or selection.

In addition to original and calibrated WGAN-DML, I report empirical-local
DML and a full-sample smoothed plug-in with the identical Gaussian kernel and
bandwidth. All roots use the same direct-inversion reference. Grid brackets
are refined numerically to tolerance $10^{-10}$. The fixed-bandwidth panel
targets $r_h$ at $h=0.15$. The exact-reserve panel applies the existing
covariance-aware Richardson combination on the $n^{-0.2}$ bandwidth path;
this remains a diagnostic rather than an exact-reserve coverage theorem.

Table~\ref{tab:calibrated-wgan} shows that the calibration reduces
fixed-bandwidth RMSE from 0.0713, 0.0654, and 0.0444 to 0.0456, 0.0182,
and 0.0080, respectively. Coverage changes from 80, 50, and 30 percent to
100, 90, and 100 percent while mean interval length falls in every case.
These are pilot estimates: even ten successes out of ten give a Wilson
lower bound of only 72.2 percent. They should not be read as evidence of
exact or conservative coverage in repeated use.

The empirical-local estimates are nearly identical to the calibrated ones.
The same-kernel smoothed plug-in has still lower fixed-bandwidth RMSE at all
three sample sizes. Thus the improvement over raw WGAN-DML establishes a
useful safeguard against its local error, but does not establish incremental
accuracy from retaining the WGAN. Paired squared-error differences and their
Monte Carlo standard errors are included in the replication archive.

For the exact-reserve diagnostic, calibrated Richardson RMSE is 0.0897,
0.0472, and 0.0172. Its observed coverage is 80, 90, and 100 percent, again
with substantial Monte Carlo uncertainty. Uncalibrated WGAN inputs become
unstable on the shrinking-bandwidth path: at $n=10000$, half of the runs
have multiple roots at one of the two bandwidths, one corrected estimate
is negative, and corrected RMSE is 0.3064. These failures are retained rather
than clipped or discarded. The calibration and both empirical comparators have no missing or
multiple roots in this pilot. These results favor the small post-fit change
over adding network or loss complexity, while retaining a clear empirical-only
benchmark and the separate exact-target validity limitation.
